
$$
U=x+jy\quad\quad|\quad\quad U^*=x-jy
$$

#### Their Real and Imaginary parts
###### real part[^real_part_verification]
$$
\text{Re}\{U\}=\tfrac{1}{2}(U+U^*)
$$

###### imaginary part[^imaginary_part_verification]
$$
\mathrm{Im}\{U\}=\tfrac{1}{2j}(U-U^*)
$$








[^real_part_verification]: $\tfrac{1}{2}(U+U^*)=\left[(x+jy)+(x-jy)\right]/2 =\tfrac{2x}{2}=x$
[^imaginary_part_verification]: $\tfrac{1}{2j}(U-U^*)=(x+jy)-(x-jy)=\tfrac{2jy}{2j}=y$
