**Appendix**
- $\exp(x)$ means $e^{x}$
- $e^{j\theta}=\cos\theta + j\sin\theta$
- $\exp[j\varphi(\mathbf{r})]$ -- a unit-magnitude rotation by the phase $\varphi$, which depends on position
- $\exp(j2\pi\nu t)$ -- a unit-magnitude rotation that spins at frequency $\nu$ as time goes on
